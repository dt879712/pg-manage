## Full description [4000]
Dev Hostel is a simple, fast way to run a PG or hostel from your phone.

Manage every part of your hostel in one place:
- Students & admissions: keep student records, rooms and guardian details together.
- Rooms & occupancy: see what is free and who is where at a glance.
- Fees & invoices: create invoices, record payments and track dues.
- Check-in / check-out: the gate records a short video when a student goes in or out.
- Parents stay informed: guardians can see their child's latest activity and footage.
- Staff & guards: role-based access keeps everyone to what they need.
- Notices & messages: send announcements and chat with parents.
- Reports: export receipts and forms as PDFs.

Built for real hostels: works on phones and tablets, with a clean light and dark theme.
